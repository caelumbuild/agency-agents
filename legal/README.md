# Caelum Build SaaS Legal Agent Team

This division provides attorney-support agents for Caelum Build's unresolved SaaS legal decisions. These agents create evidence-backed decision packages, draft alternatives, conflict reports, and document-propagation plans. They do **not** provide legal advice, make final legal decisions, or replace licensed counsel.

## Required attorney decisions covered

1. Final legal entity and address
2. Formation and operating jurisdictions
3. Governing law and venue
4. Arbitration and class-action waiver
5. Liability cap
6. Insurance alignment
7. Incident-notification period
8. Data-retention periods
9. Service levels and uptime promises
10. Supported countries
11. Final subprocessor list
12. International transfer mechanisms
13. Renewal and refund terms
14. Exact security commitments
15. Final DPA applicability
16. Customer contracting model

## Recommended operating sequence

1. The Decision Coordinator opens the 16-item docket and identifies missing facts.
2. Corporate/Jurisdiction establishes entity, operating geography, contractual forum, and dispute options.
3. Privacy/DPA maps data flows, vendors, retention reality, countries, and transfer dependencies.
4. Security/Incident validates technical evidence and bounds security, notice, and service commitments.
5. Commercial Terms develops liability, subscription, renewal, refund, remedy, and SLA positions.
6. Customer Contracting integrates the approved positions into contracting paths and insurance review.
7. The Decision Coordinator produces the attorney agenda and tracks final decisions through every affected document.

## Output controls

Every material statement must be labeled as a verified fact, operating assumption, proposed business position, attorney decision required, or implementation dependency. Unapproved variables remain explicit placeholders. No agent may silently insert a market-standard term as a Caelum commitment.

## Attorney handoff package

The consolidated package should contain:
- executive decision dashboard
- one decision record per unresolved item
- factual evidence and missing-fact requests
- alternatives and commercial consequences
- technical and insurance dependencies
- cross-document contradiction report
- proposed business position for counsel review
- counsel questions
- final approval and propagation checklist

## Repository boundary

Do not store privileged legal advice, signed agreements, customer-confidential documents, credentials, tax identifiers, insurance policy numbers, or other sensitive records in this public agent repository.
